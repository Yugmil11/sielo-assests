Freedom for you

Handle daily tasks yourself and rely less on caregivers

01 / Reach

Pick up objects, independently.

ARLO reaches, grips, and lifts everyday items on command, so you're not waiting on someone else to hand them to you.

"Being able to grab my phone off the table myself, whenever I want, sounds small until you've had to wait 20 minutes for someone to walk by."

Alex R., 29 — Cerebral Palsy

02 / Drink

Drink, independently.

Bring your own cup to your mouth, on your own schedule, whenever you're thirsty.

"I used to time how much water I drank around when someone was free to help. Now I just drink when I'm thirsty, like anyone else."

Hollis Peirce

03 / Eat

Eat, independently.

Fork food from your plate to your mouth, bite after bite, without a caregiver feeding you.

"Dinner used to be rushed because someone else had somewhere to be. Now I eat at my own pace, and it actually feels like my meal again."

Izzy
